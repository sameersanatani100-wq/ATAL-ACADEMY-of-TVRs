About the academy

TVR's Academy started as a single classroom and grew because parents kept asking for the same thing: smaller batches, real attention, and teachers who explain a concept as many times as it takes. That's still how every class here is run.

We keep batch sizes deliberately small, track each student's progress through the term rather than just before exams, and stay in regular touch with parents — not only at report card time.
